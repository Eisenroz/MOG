Curatorial Statement

MOG (Memable Online Gallery) explores the relationship between historical artworks and contemporary internet meme culture. Works of art have always been used to communicate ideas, emotions, and social commentary. While some have critical contexts and messages behind each piece, they can be reinterpreted and recontexualized as they circulate through different eyes and eras.

This gallery brings together hand selected artworks from the Rijksmuseum that bear striking resemblence to popular memes. Through similiarities in composition, gesture, and expression. MOG examines how historical imagery can take on new meanings when viewed through the lens of contemporary internet culture.
